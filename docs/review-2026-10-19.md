What this change does: fixes three problems found while reviewing everything merged
to `main` from #42 to #54 (15–19 October), and sets the bugfix version to 0.34.1.

This review covered the full diff `2b4e891..3aab9a0`: controller support, macOS
packaging, vehicle takeoff, slope and traversal collision, LOD chains, mixed-opacity
batches, the native UI refresh, and the deferred first-area upload with texture
deduplication. It is a correctness review plus a Ponytail Review pass for needless
complexity. The assumed load is one native client streaming the existing bounded
regions within the 20-player session limit.

## Must fix — resolved

1. **A session started during the first area upload loses collision** (`native/crates/runtime/src/main.rs`, `stream_world`)
   - **What this is:** Since #54 the first map area uploads to the GPU over several
     frames. Its collision and water are installed at once, so the finished upload
     must not replace them.
   - **Problem:** That rule was a `State` flag, but hosting or joining a session swaps
     the offline world (including its pending upload) out of `State` and leaves the
     flag behind. The session's first region then installed without collision and
     water, and when the offline world came back, its upload replaced the offline
     collision with nothing.
   - **Fix:** The upload itself now carries the rule (`Upload::batches_only`), so it
     moves with the world it belongs to. The `State` flag is removed.
   - **If we skip it:** Joining from the launcher while the first area is still
     uploading can leave the player walking on the wrong collision or falling
     through the map after leaving the session.

2. **Smoke captures with a fixed camera rendered an empty world** (`main.rs`, sign/neon/culling captures)
   - **Problem:** Those captures remove the streamer, which is also what advances the
     deferred first upload, so its batches never appeared.
   - **Fix:** `State::finish_upload_now` completes the pending upload before the
     streamer is removed.

3. **Opaque and transparent variants of one texture were decoded twice** (`native/crates/scene/src/lib.rs`, `load_area`)
   - **Problem:** Since #48, `txd:foo` and `txd:foo|alpha` are separate batches.
     The decoded image was stored only under whichever key came first; when that was
     the `|alpha` or UV-animated variant, the base texture was read and decoded again.
   - **Fix:** A per-load cache keyed by the base texture name. This removes CPU work
     and memory on the streaming thread, which matters most on weak PCs.

Also fixed: the upload log paired a whole-frame peak time with the name of a single
slower operation; it now reports `frame slice` when the frame total is the peak.

## Reviewed, not changed

- LOD-of-LOD suppression (`covered_lods`) hides a coarse LOD when any child is drawn.
  Children beyond 2.5 km or cut by the placement budget could leave a far ring of
  missing terrain. This needs an original-map check before changing.
- `resolve_body` now treats every walkable slope (normal Y ≥ 0.55) as ground, not
  wall, for both players and cars. This is the intended 0.33.1 behaviour.
- Ponytail pass: the per-operation timing in `Upload::advance` (seven call sites) and
  the 340-line `--probe-curbs-ramps` tool in `main.rs` are diagnostic code that could
  be shortened or moved to its own module. Not a correctness issue.

## Verification

- All 321 Rust workspace tests pass; `cargo fmt --check` and Clippy with warnings
  denied pass for the workspace and all targets.
- Not checked: GPU rendering, joining a real session during the first upload, and
  original-map gameplay. These fixes have no automated GPU test.

Verdict: Ship.
