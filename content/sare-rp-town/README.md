# SARE RP Town

An original low-poly roleplay town for SARE (340 × 340 m): police station
with cells, hospital with helipad, bank, 24/7 shop, gas station, City Hall,
Premium Motors car dealer, mechanic garage, public garage, taxi depot,
apartments, houses, a warehouse yard, a park with a fountain, street lights
and an invisible wall at the edge (`COL_EdgeWall`).

It is the map of the **SARE RP Town** server template
(`sv_template sare-rp`); see
[server-data/resources/[sare-rp]/README.md](../../server-data/resources/[sare-rp]/README.md)
for hosting and joining. On its own you can walk and drive around it:

```text
sa-runtime --content sare-rp-town
```

Everything is generated by
[`tools/blender/make_rp_town.py`](../../tools/blender/make_rp_town.py) in
Blender 4.2+, including the small textures; there are no third-party assets.
Licence: CC0-1.0 (public domain).

```text
blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/blender/make_rp_town.py
```

rebuilds `world.glb` and the server's `locations.lua` and spawn points.
Signs are `NOCOL_` (not solid); glass is see-through and not solid.
